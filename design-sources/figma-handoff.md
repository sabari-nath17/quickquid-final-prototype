## Source of truth

The image layers are implementation captures from the registered prototype routes. The matching source files are in `implementation/`, with WebP copies in `implementation-webp/` and route metadata in `manifest.json`. The FigJam map is the navigation and ownership reference; use the route names and source filenames rather than inventing new screens.
