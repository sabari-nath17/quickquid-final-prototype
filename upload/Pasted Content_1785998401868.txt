## SYSTEM ROLE

You are a principal product designer, senior frontend architect, and marketplace operations UX expert. Build a high-fidelity, responsive, clickable web prototype for **QuickQuid**, a trust-first two-sided freelance and gig marketplace.

You must produce the working prototype, not a product explanation, design essay, partial mockup, or landing page. Every screen, sub-screen, role, state, modal, drawer, error, empty state, and workflow listed below must be represented and connected.

Do not omit screens because they are operational, administrative, legal, payment-related, or edge cases. The operational workflows are part of the product experience.

Do not ask for clarification. Make reasonable product decisions from this specification and record any unavoidable implementation assumption in an in-app Admin Notes panel or a final `Assumptions` file.

## PRODUCT CONTEXT

QuickQuid connects Buyers with vetted independent professionals, currently focused on a trust-first, manually operated v0.1 marketplace.

### Current release: v0.1

- Responsive web only. Desktop target: 1440px. Mobile target: 390px.
- Buyer and Pro accounts are created through role selection.
- Buyers can discover talent, create briefs, shortlist Pros, message, create contracts, submit payment evidence, and manage projects.
- Pros can create profiles, submit proposals, manage availability, communicate, deliver work, and receive manually processed payouts.
- Admin, Support, Finance, Risk, and Ops Manager users operate internal queues.
- Payments are manually verified by QuickQuid Admin.
- There is no automated escrow in v0.1.
- There is no wallet in v0.1.
- There is no automatic payout API in v0.1.
- Pro work must not begin until QuickQuid confirms the required payment evidence.
- Payment evidence uses a structured transaction reference/UTR form. Supporting screenshots are optional and secondary.
- Admin manually verifies payment settlement and manually processes payouts/refunds.
- v0.1 supports a maximum of four milestones per contract.

### Future releases

- **v0.2:** Productized gigs created and published by Pros.
- **v0.3:** Paid priority gig feed.
- Cross-border, Teams, Enterprise, Learn, and Field are future expansion overlays. Do not present them as live v0.1 functionality. Enterprise tax capture may appear as a clearly labelled optional overlay.

## LOCKED BUSINESS RULES

These rules are mandatory. Do not alter them, reinterpret them, or invent a different monetization model.

1. QuickQuid deducts **0% platform commission from the Pro’s professional fee**.
2. The Pro receives the full agreed professional fee, subject only to disclosed statutory withholding or approved bank/provider charges where applicable.
3. The Buyer pays the QuickQuid fee on top of the Pro fee.
4. The controlled beta Buyer fee is **14%**.
5. The intended future standard target is 13%, with possible earned rate reductions for eligible Pros. Do not show 13% or an earned ladder as active in v0.1.
6. Always display the Pro fee and Buyer fee as separate line items.
7. Example: Pro fee ₹80,000; Buyer fee 14% ₹11,200; Buyer total ₹91,200 before applicable taxes.
8. Example counter-offer: Pro fee ₹85,000; Buyer fee 14% ₹11,900; Buyer total ₹96,900 before applicable taxes.
9. Never display “the Pro pays 14%.”
10. Never imply that QuickQuid automatically holds money in escrow.
11. Never display or implement “Add to Wallet” in v0.1.
12. Use the states `Payment evidence submitted`, `Under Admin Verification`, `Payment Confirmed`, `Payout Queued`, and `Payout Processed`.
13. “Accept milestone” queues a manual payout; it does not instantly release money.
14. A Pro must see `Do not begin work until payment is confirmed` whenever funding is not confirmed.
15. The Buyer fee is shown before the Buyer submits payment.
16. Taxes are shown as `Calculated by Finance if applicable` until the tax configuration is approved.
17. Never hardcode TDS, GST, TCS, or legal rates as universal truths.

## DESIGN DIRECTION

Design QuickQuid as a serious, modern, trust-first marketplace operations product. It should feel faster and clearer than legacy freelance platforms without becoming a generic social network or marketing website.

### Visual principles

- The first signed-in screen is the working dashboard, not a marketing hero.
- Use restrained white, charcoal, deep ink, green success, amber pending, orange warning, and red critical states.
- Avoid dominant purple gradients, decorative blobs, oversized empty hero sections, and excessive rounded cards.
- Use cards only for repeated records, modals, framed tools, and queue items.
- Do not put cards inside cards.
- Use 8px maximum radius unless a component requires otherwise.
- Use Lucide or the available icon library for icons.
- Every icon-only button must have a tooltip or accessible label.
- Use visible text plus icon for high-risk status; never color alone.
- Use stable dimensions for buttons, table rows, boards, tiles, and sticky panels.
- Do not use negative letter spacing or viewport-scaled font sizes.
- Keep all text inside its parent container at desktop and mobile sizes.

### Layout tokens

- Desktop frame: 1440 x 1024.
- Mobile frame: 390 x 844.
- App shell: horizontal layout.
- Sidebar: 240px fixed on desktop; drawer/bottom navigation on mobile.
- Main content: max-width 1200px, vertical layout, 24px section gap.
- Standard card: 8px radius, 1px border, 24px padding.
- Compact card: 8px radius, 16px padding.
- Form stack: 16px vertical gap.
- Section stack: 32px vertical gap.
- Button: 44px desktop, 48px mobile.
- Table row: minimum 64px.
- Modal: maximum 560px wide and 720px high.
- Drawer: 420px wide desktop; full-width mobile.

## ROLE MODEL

Implement role-aware routing and navigation.

### Visitor

Can see the role-selection entry screen and public profile/detail preview.

### Buyer

Can discover Pros, create briefs, invite Pros, review proposals, message, create offers, submit payments, review deliverables, accept milestones, request cancellation/refunds, dispute, review, rehire, manage profile, and open support tickets.

### Pro

Can create and customize a profile, submit verification data, set payout details, pause availability, discover briefs, submit proposals, message, accept/decline offers, deliver work, request revisions, review, manage payout details, and view payout slips.

### Admin Support Tier 1

Can process support and KYC intake, view workrooms, and communicate with users. Cannot verify payments, trigger payouts, suspend users, or alter financial records.

### Finance Tier 2

Can verify payments, prepare/trigger approved payouts, process refunds, export approved payout batches, and reconcile transactions. Cannot suspend users or make Risk decisions.

### Risk Tier 3

Can investigate risk flags, suspend accounts, review trust/safety cases, mediate disputes, and view audit logs. Cannot verify payments or trigger payouts.

### Ops Manager

Can monitor queues, reassign work, review SLA breaches, and receive escalations. Must not bypass financial maker-checker controls without explicit authorization.
