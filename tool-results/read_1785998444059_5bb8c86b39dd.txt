    19→    19→## PRODUCT CONTEXT
    20→    20→
    21→    21→QuickQuid connects Buyers with vetted independent professionals, currently focused on a trust-first, manually operated v0.1 marketplace.
    22→    22→
    23→    23→### Current release: v0.1
    24→    24→
    25→    25→- Responsive web only. Desktop target: 1440px. Mobile target: 390px.
    26→    26→- Buyer and Pro accounts are created through role selection.
    27→    27→- Buyers can discover talent, create briefs, shortlist Pros, message, create contracts, submit payment evidence, and manage projects.
    28→    28→- Pros can create profiles, submit proposals, manage availability, communicate, deliver work, and receive manually processed payouts.
    29→    29→- Admin, Support, Finance, Risk, and Ops Manager users operate internal queues.
    30→    30→- Payments are manually verified by QuickQuid Admin.
    31→    31→- There is no automated escrow in v0.1.
    32→    32→- There is no wallet in v0.1.
    33→    33→- There is no automatic payout API in v0.1.
    34→    34→- Pro work must not begin until QuickQuid confirms the required payment evidence.
    35→    35→- Payment evidence uses a structured transaction reference/UTR form. Supporting screenshots are optional and secondary.
    36→    36→- Admin manually verifies payment settlement and manually processes payouts/refunds.
    37→    37→- v0.1 supports a maximum of four milestones per contract.
    38→    38→
    39→    39→### Future releases
    40→    40→
    41→    41→- **v0.2:** Productized gigs created and published by Pros.
    42→    42→- **v0.3:** Paid priority gig feed.
    43→    43→- Cross-border, Teams, Enterprise, Learn, and Field are future expansion overlays. Do not present them as live v0.1 functionality. Enterprise tax capture may appear as a clearly labelled optional overlay.

    45→    45→## LOCKED BUSINESS RULES
    46→    46→
    47→    47→These rules are mandatory. Do not alter them, reinterpret them, or invent a different monetization model.
    48→    48→
    49→    49→1. QuickQuid deducts **0% platform commission from the Pro’s professional fee**.
    50→    50→2. The Pro receives the full agreed professional fee, subject only to disclosed statutory withholding or approved bank/provider charges where applicable.
    51→    51→3. The Buyer pays the QuickQuid fee on top of the Pro fee.
    52→    52→4. The controlled beta Buyer fee is **14%**.
    53→    53→5. The intended future standard target is 13%, with possible earned rate reductions for eligible Pros. Do not show 13% or an earned ladder as active in v0.1.
    54→    54→6. Always display the Pro fee and Buyer fee as separate line items.
    55→    55→7. Example: Pro fee ₹80,000; Buyer fee 14% ₹11,200; Buyer total ₹91,200 before applicable taxes.
    56→    56→8. Example counter-offer: Pro fee ₹85,000; Buyer fee 14% ₹11,900; Buyer total ₹96,900 before applicable taxes.
    57→    57→9. Never display “the Pro pays 14%.”
    58→    58→10. Never imply that QuickQuid automatically holds money in escrow.
    59→    59→11. Never display or implement “Add to Wallet” in v0.1.
    60→    60→12. Use the states `Payment evidence submitted`, `Under Admin Verification`, `Payment Confirmed`, `Payout Queued`, and `Payout Processed`.
    61→    61→13. “Accept milestone” queues a manual payout; it does not instantly release money.
    62→    62→14. A Pro must see `Do not begin work until payment is confirmed` whenever funding is not confirmed.
    63→    63→15. The Buyer fee is shown before the Buyer submits payment.
    64→    64→16. Taxes are shown as `Calculated by Finance if applicable` until the tax configuration is approved.
    65→    65→17. Never hardcode TDS, GST, TCS, or legal rates as universal truths.
